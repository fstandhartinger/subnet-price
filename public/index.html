HOW TO READ THIS

A view of the mechanism.

Cost & registrations

The line tracks the network registration cost. Red points mark observed registrations and their estimated prices paid. After a registration the cost jumps; between registrations it decays.

Conservative band & uncertainty buffer

The grey range spans the last eight registration prices. The blue conservative band follows their robust trend, anchored to recent prices and deliberately biased towards higher prices and earlier registrations. Its width uses the residual spread, with a minimum of 30% of the trend value (the equivalent of ±15%, shifted upward). The lighter outer band adds uncertainty using residual standard deviation or the 95th-percentile excess, with a minimum extra 15% margin. These are planning buffers, not statistical confidence intervals. To be safe, budget for the upper edge of the buffer.

A conditional path

The dashed line extends the current decay, assuming no new registration and no change to the mechanism. The safe estimate marks the first touch of the lighter buffer’s upper edge, assuming competitors register earlier; if that threshold is already reached, it says so. Dated markers also show the grey range crossing. A registration resets that path. The chain has a minimum cost floor; an endpoint shown before that floor is available is illustrative. The projection is limited to 60 days. This is a mechanism illustration, not advice.
